Core concepts

Three ideas, one exciting field.

These concepts are related, but they are not exactly the same. Here is a quick introduction.

✦

Artificial Intelligence

AI is a broad field focused on building computer systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as reasoning or understanding language.

▥

Machine Learning

Machine learning uses data to train models that identify patterns and make predictions, rather than relying only on manually written rules.

◎

Deep Learning

Deep learning is a type of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from data.

Real-world impact

Where can AIML be used?

AI and machine learning support many industries. Their results depend on the quality of data, careful evaluation and responsible use.

Examples of applications

  • Healthcare: supporting analysis of medical images and research.
  • Transport: estimating travel time and helping plan routes.
  • Education: adapting practice material to learners' needs.
  • Agriculture: analysing crop images and weather data.
  • Accessibility: speech recognition and text-to-speech tools.

Responsible AI matters

AI systems can make errors or reflect bias in their data. Good projects consider privacy, fairness, security, accessibility and human oversight.

Key idea: Use AI as a tool to assist people, and check its output carefully.
